## How to play
Like in the original Battle Network series, you operate a NetNavi to fight viruses on a 3x6 grid using BattleChips. However, in Net Invaders there are three unqiue twists to the battle mechanics:

**1.  NetNavi mobility:**
In Battle Network, both NetNavi and viruses can move in their own 3x3 grid. In Net Invaders, NetNavis can only move up or down on a 1x3 grid or a single column rather.

**2. Virus attacking patterns:**
In Battle Network, each virus has its own patttern to attack MegaMan and deplete his hit points. In Net Invaders, viruses do not need to attack NetNavis directly. They deplete their hit points by reaching the NetNavi's 1x3 grid. Therefore, viruses do not attack but travel the grid with different speed and durability trying to reach its end.

**3. Permanent BattleChip Boosts**
In Battle Network, BattleChips are unleash powerful attacks when activated manually. In Net Invaders, first of all BattleChips are called BoostChips. But while their selection underlies similar mechanics, instead of unleashing powerful single attacks, BoostChips grant permanent buffs, improving a NetNavis auto-shoot capabilities to defeat the endless waves of incoming viruses.
